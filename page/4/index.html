Meet Savos:

Savos orchestrates your hiring process with specialized AI agents,
keeping recruiters, hiring managers, and candidates aligned at every step.

How Savos Aligns Every Step of Hiring

Behind every faster, smarter hire is a team of three AI agents working together through Savos Intelligence, the orchestration engine that keeps hiring in sync. Each agent is designed to support recruiters, hiring managers, and candidates while staying connected through one intelligent system.
HR Leaders
Hiring Managers
Candidates

For Hiring Managers
The Decision Advisor

  • Delivers pre-interview candidate reports with structured insights.
  • Reviews strengths, role fit, red flags, and real-time interview question suggestions.
  • Cuts redundant interview rounds and accelerates decisions.
  • Focuses on interviewing the right people, not more people.
Let’s Get Started

For Recruiters
The Architect of Alignment

  • Configures AI-driven scorecards for every role.
  • Builds custom candidate reports and automates feedback loops.
  • Handles resume parsing, summaries, and progress updates.
  • Frees recruiters to focus on strategy, alignment, and results.
Show Me How It Works

For Candidates
The Guided Experience

  • Acts as a single AI-guided touchpoint that creates a consistent, engaging experience.
  • Provides timely updates and personalized guidance at every stage.
  • Ensures transparent communication and fair evaluation.
  • Keeps candidates valued and connected throughout the process.
